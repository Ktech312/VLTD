# VLTD — Brand Positioning & Philosophy
*The foundation document. Read this before writing any copy, building any feature, or talking to any press.*

---

## What VLTD Is

VLTD (pronounced "Vaulted") is the central command for serious collectors. Not a marketplace. Not a social network. Not another single-category card tracker. A vault — private, secure, yours — that happens to connect to everything else.

Collectors trust VLTD with their most prized possessions: the things they've spent years and thousands of dollars building. That trust is the product. Everything we build either earns more of it or doesn't ship.

---

## The Philosophy: Trust Over Tethering

Every other app in this space wants to own your collection. They want you logging in to their marketplace, buying their premium tier, locked into their ecosystem. They make money when you're dependent.

We make money when you trust us. That's a fundamentally different bet.

**What this means in practice:**
- Your vault is private by default. You share what you want, when you want, to whoever you choose.
- We help you sell on eBay, Whatnot, Heritage, Discogs — not just on VLTD. Export anywhere. Leave anytime.
- We never charge you to access your own data. Export is always free.
- We never show you ads. We never sell your collection data.
- We never make you feel caged.

The pitch is simple: **Store it here for free. Track its value. Document it for insurance. And when you're ready to sell, we'll help you post it anywhere with one click.**

Users who feel free stay forever. Users who feel trapped leave the moment a better option appears.

## Priority Stack

This order matters. Every product decision should be evaluated against it.

**1. The Vault** — Private inventory, insurance documentation, personal record keeping. This is why someone trusts us with 1,000 cards or a $40,000 graded comic. The vault has to feel like a bank: secure, organized, and completely in their control. This is the reason VLTD exists.

**2. The Gallery** — The collector's pride. Being able to see your entire collection beautifully organized, track what it's worth, know which items are notable, document condition and provenance. This is what makes the vault worth building.

**3. The Share** — Controlled social. Share a single item. Share a curated showcase of 10. Keep everything else locked. Never forced, never all-or-nothing. This is how VLTD becomes a social object — not by making everything public, but by making sharing feel safe and intentional.

**4. The Sale** — When trust is established and the collector is ready, VLTD helps them sell. Not by building another marketplace (not yet), but by generating the listing copy, the optimized photos, the item specs — and pushing it to wherever they already sell. We're the launchpad, not the landing zone.

## Voice & Tone

**What VLTD sounds like:**
- Confident, not arrogant. We know what we're building.
- Warm, not corporate. Collectors are passionate people. We respect that.
- Direct. No filler words. No buzzwords. Say the thing.
- Knowledgeable. We speak collector. We know what PSA 10 means, what a first edition is, why condition is everything.
- Never pushy. We don't upsell. We don't guilt. We don't FOMO.

**What VLTD never sounds like:**
- Startup-speak ("disruptive," "ecosystem," "leverage synergies")
- Condescending ("even non-collectors can use this!")
- Desperate ("Sign up NOW before the beta closes!")
- Vague ("The best way to manage your collection")

**The test:** Would a serious collector with 10 years of experience read this and think "these people get it"? If yes, ship it. If not, rewrite.

## The Competitive Position

Every competitor is single-category. CLZ does comics. CollX does sports cards. Shiny does TCG. Discogs does vinyl. TCDB does baseball cards.

The serious collector doesn't just collect one thing. They have cards AND comics AND vinyl AND games. They're using 4 apps and a spreadsheet and still falling through the cracks.

VLTD is the first vault that holds everything.

**Our unfair advantages:**
1. **Multi-category** — One vault for every universe. Nobody else does this.
2. **Insurance-grade documentation** — PDF reports with photos, condition, value, provenance. Nobody does this well.
3. **Privacy-first** — Most apps are built around sharing. We're built around owning. Different foundation, different trust.
4. **Open ecosystem** — We help you sell anywhere. Nobody else helps you leave.
5. **Gallery quality** — Museum view, stream mode, cinematic item reveals. The collection looks as good as it deserves to.

## Who VLTD Is For

**The Primary Collector**
- Age 22–50, any income bracket, any geography
- Owns $500–$500,000 in collectibles across 1–5 categories
- Currently uses: spreadsheets, 2–3 category-specific apps, or nothing
- Pain: "Nothing covers all my stuff. I don't know what my collection is actually worth. I have no way to document it for insurance."
- What they want: one place, beautiful, organized, private, trustworthy

**The Casual Builder**
- Just getting started. 50–200 items. Doesn't have a "system" yet.
- They'll grow into power users if VLTD is where they start
- The free tier is specifically for them — no credit card, no commitment, no ceiling on adding items

**The Content Creator Collector**
- Has a TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram about their collection
- Wants beautiful item reveals, stream mode for live openings, haul recaps
- Shareability is their primary need, but they still want the vault as the backbone

**The Insurance-Minded Collector**
- Homeowner with serious collection value — $10K–$1M+
- Needs documentation: photos, condition, provenance, current value
- Would pay for this functionality. Serious professional use case.

## What VLTD Is Not

Getting this wrong in copy or product is an instant trust-killer.

- **Not a marketplace.** We don't take a cut of your sales. We help you sell elsewhere.
- **Not a social network.** You don't need followers. You don't need likes. Your collection doesn't owe anyone visibility.
- **Not an investment advisor.** We track value. We don't tell you what to buy or sell.
- **Not a grading service.** We document condition using AI and let you override. We don't authenticate.
- **Not a subscription trap.** Free tier is genuinely useful. Paid tier adds power, not access to your own data.

## Intellectual Property Notes
*(Not legal advice — consult a trademark attorney before launch)*

Before going public with the VLTD name and brand, the following should be evaluated:

**Trademark search — do this first:**
- Search "VLTD" at USPTO.gov (TESS database) to confirm no existing registration in Class 9 (software/apps) or Class 35 (business services/inventory management)
- Search "Vaulted" — this word is more likely to have conflicts. Narrow to your specific classes.
- International: if launch is US-only first, file in the US. WIPO Madrid Protocol covers international expansion later.

**What to consider filing:**
- The wordmark "VLTD" — the name itself
- The wordmark "Vaulted" if used as the spoken/full name
- The logo/icon if you have a distinctive mark

**Other IP steps before launch:**
- Reserve the app name on App Store and Google Play early (even before submission)
- Lock down social handles: @vltd and @vaultedapp across Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube now
- Domain: vltd.app, vltd.io, getvltd.com — own the name in multiple TLDs before someone else does after you get press
- Copyright for original content (landing page copy, marketing materials) is automatic upon creation in the US, but registration with the Copyright Office ($45–65) creates a legal record and enables statutory damages if someone copies your work

**Timeline suggestion:**
- File trademark application before any press coverage or public launch announcement
- USPTO processing takes 8–12 months, but the filing date is your legal priority date — so filing early matters even if approval takes time
- Budget $300–$1,500 for a trademark attorney to do a proper clearance search and file the application correctly
