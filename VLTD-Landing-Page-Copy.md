## Section 6 — Features Highlight

**Section label:** WHAT'S INSIDE

**Headline:**
## Built for the way collectors actually think.

**Feature cards (6 total):**

**AI Condition Grading**
Point your camera. VLTD reads the condition and suggests a grade — PSA scale for cards, CGC scale for comics, Goldmine for vinyl. You confirm or override. Documented either way.

**Insurance Documentation**
One tap generates a print-ready PDF with every item: photo, title, condition, grade, estimated value, purchase date. The kind of documentation your insurance company actually wants.

**Museum View**
Your vault, displayed like a gallery. Spotlight your most notable piece. Browse by universe. Every item gets the presentation it deserves. Not a list. Not a grid. A collection.

**Stream Mode**
Fullscreen cinematic reveal for live collectors. Tap to reveal your item and its value — designed for TikTok, Reels, and Twitch. Gold value reveal. Your audience loses it.

**Portfolio Intelligence**
Real-time valuation across your entire vault. Biggest movers. Best ROI. Net if sold today. The financial layer that turns your hobby into something you can actually track.

**Haul Mode**
Coming soon. Rapid-fire scan at conventions and shows. Keep your camera open, scan item after item, review the haul at the end. Built for the collector who adds 50 items in a weekend.

## Section 7 — Privacy Section

**Section label:** PRIVATE BY DESIGN

**Headline:**
## Your collection is yours. We're just the vault.

**Body:**
VLTD was built privacy-first, not social-first. Your vault is completely private unless you actively choose to share something. There are no public feeds, no follower counts, no pressure to post.

Every item has a lock. Use it.

When you do share — a single item, a curated showcase, a stream reveal — you control exactly what's visible. Value, financials, location, condition notes: share all of it, some of it, or none of it.

**Three promises:**
- 🚫 We never show you ads
- 🚫 We never sell your collection data
- 🚫 We never make your vault public without your permission
