# Vaulted — Language Guide: Artists vs. Collectors

---

## The Core Rule

**For ARTISTS:** Never say "AI." Say what it does, not what powers it.
**For COLLECTORS / Comics / Cards / Memorabilia:** AI is a selling point. Use it freely.

Artists are protective of their creative identity — and rightfully so. The AI art controversy
has made many feel like technology is against them. Vaulted is actually the opposite (it
protects their work, not replaces it) but you won't get the chance to explain that if the
word "AI" shuts them down in the first sentence.

Let them discover what powers it later, once they already love what it does for them.

---

## Word Swap Reference

| Instead of this (AI language) | Say this (artist-safe language) |
|---|---|
| AI catalogs your artwork | automatically documents your artwork |
| AI object recognition | smart subject detection |
| AI-powered platform | digital documentation tool |
| AI authentication | digital fingerprinting |
| AI scans your certificate | reads your certificate automatically |
| Powered by machine learning | built-in smart scanning |
| AI background removal | automatic background cleanup |
| AI identifies your piece | automatically builds your record |
| Certificate generated by AI | system-generated certificate |
| AI art tool | art documentation tool |

## How to Talk to Artists About What Vaulted Does

Focus on these three things — in this order:

**1. Protection** — "It protects your credit. If someone ever questions who made it, you have proof."
**2. Record** — "It's a real record of your body of work — automatically built as you go."
**3. Simplicity** — "You take a photo. It does the rest."

Never lead with features. Lead with what the artist *gets*.

---

## Artist-Safe Feature Descriptions

**Instead of:** "AI object recognition automatically identifies your artwork"
**Say:** "Takes one photo and builds a complete catalog entry — subject matter, medium, dimensions"

**Instead of:** "AI authentication creates a digital fingerprint"
**Say:** "Creates a unique digital record that's tied to your piece — like a fingerprint for your art"

**Instead of:** "AI OCR reads your certificates"
**Say:** "Scans any certificates, labels, or notes you already have and adds them to the record automatically"

**Instead of:** "AI background removal"
**Say:** "Cleans up the background automatically so every archive photo looks professional"

---

## How to Talk to Collectors / Comics / Cards / Memorabilia

These audiences WANT to hear about technology. It signals legitimacy and precision.

Lead with:
- "AI-verified catalog entry"
- "Smart authentication built in"
- "AI scans and records every detail automatically"
- "Machine-verified certificate of authenticity"
- "Digital fingerprint powered by computer vision"

Collectors care about value, authenticity, and having something they can show a buyer.
Technology = trust in this context. Lean all the way in.

## The Two Homepages (Conceptually)

Consider having two landing page versions eventually — or at minimum, two different
sections/headlines depending on who's reading:

**Artist version headline:**
> "Your art deserves a real record. We built the easiest way to create one."

**Collector version headline:**
> "AI-verified certificates of authenticity for every piece in your collection."

Same product. Two completely different conversations.
