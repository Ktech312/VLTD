## SEQUENCE NOTES (internal)

**Tone throughout:** Knowledgeable and direct. Not hype. Not pushy. Speaks to the collector as a peer — someone who takes their collection seriously.

**What these emails DON'T do (by design):**
- Don't push premium/upsell yet (that's a separate sequence)
- Don't overwhelm with feature lists
- Don't use words like "seamlessly," "unlock," "leverage"
- Don't lead with the app's features — lead with the collector's situation

**Trigger logic:**
- Email 1: Immediately on signup
- Email 2: Day 2 OR when first item is added (whichever is sooner — adapt the copy)
- Email 3: Day 4 (send regardless of item count)
- Email 4: Day 6 (send regardless)
- Email 5: Day 8 (send regardless)

**A/B test candidates:**
- Email 1 subject: "Your vault is ready." vs "Welcome to VLTD." — test open rate
- Email 2 subject: "What's it actually worth?" vs "The number most collectors don't know" — test curiosity vs pain
- Email 4 CTA: "See how it works" vs "Sell from your vault" — test action vs education framing
