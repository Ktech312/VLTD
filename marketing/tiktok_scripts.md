**Format notes:**
- Shoot vertical (9:16), phone is fine
- Each video is 30–60 seconds max
- Captions ON — most people watch with sound off
- Hook is everything — you have 1.5 seconds before someone scrolls

## POSTING STRATEGY (Zero Budget)

**Week 1:** Post Video 3 (founder story) — personal, no editing needed, just talk
**Week 2:** Post Video 1 (the problem hook) — can be pure text/screen record
**Week 3:** Post Video 2 (the demo) — screen record of the actual app
**Week 4:** Post Video 4 (did you know) — batch these, easy to make multiples

**Post time:** 6–9pm your local time on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday.

**Comment on 5–10 artist TikToks per day** before you post your own. Be genuine. This signals to the algorithm you're active and gets you on the radar of potential users.

**When a video gets traction:** Reply to EVERY comment in the first hour. This is the most important thing you can do for reach.
