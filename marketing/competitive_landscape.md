## THE BIG PICTURE

No existing app does what Vaulted does across all four categories.

Every competitor owns one lane:
- Art apps → inventory management, no smart scanning, no CoA
- Comics apps → barcode scanning + value tracking, no CoA, no provenance
- Card apps → AI-powered scanning + pricing, no CoA, no private vault
- Memorabilia → no dedicated app at all. Authentication is physical-only (PSA, JSA, Beckett)

**Vaulted's open lane: the only mobile-first app that scans any collectible, auto-catalogs it, generates a certificate of authenticity, and keeps everything in a private encrypted vault — across all categories.**

## CATEGORY 1 — ARTISTS / ART DOCUMENTATION

### Artwork Archive
- **Pricing:** $8–$16/month (Artist tiers) up to $139/month (Organization)
- **Strengths:** Well-reviewed, affordable, has CoA export, provenance tracking, private viewing rooms, sales tracking. The current market leader for independent artists.
- **Weaknesses:** Desktop-first. Manual entry — you type everything in. No photo scanning or auto-cataloging. No smart document reading. Feels like a spreadsheet with a gallery view.
- **Vaulted's edge:** Photograph it → it catalogs itself. Vaulted takes 90 seconds; Artwork Archive takes 10+ minutes per piece.

### CatalogIt
- **Pricing:** $45–$96/month. Free tier: 50 items only.
- **Strengths:** Museum-grade, cloud-based, mobile access, strong support.
- **Weaknesses:** Priced for institutions, not independent artists. No smart scanning. Overkill for a solo artist or small collector.
- **Vaulted's edge:** $9/month vs $45/month. Built for individuals, not museums.

### Artlogic / WrkLst / Art Binder
- **Pricing:** Enterprise / gallery-tier ($100–$500+/month range)
- Built for commercial galleries managing hundreds of works for sale.
- Zero relevance to independent artists or collectors.

### The Real Competitor: "Nothing" / Spreadsheets
Most working artists track their work in:
- A Google Sheet they stopped updating in 2023
- A notes app with photo dumps
- A physical binder
- Nothing at all

This is Vaulted's actual competition — inertia and the gap it fills is enormous.

## CATEGORY 2 — COMIC BOOK COLLECTORS

### CLZ Comics
- **Pricing:** $19.95/year (~$1.67/month)
- **Strengths:** Best barcode scanner in the category (99% claimed accuracy), cloud sync, value tracking via CovrPrice, large established user base. Works for comics, movies, games, books — but separate apps for each.
- **Weaknesses:** Zero certificate of authenticity. Zero provenance documentation. Zero legal-grade record. It's a cataloging database, not a vault. Does not support original comic art at all (only published issues with barcodes).
- **Vaulted's edge:** CoA generation. Provenance records. Original art documentation (which CLZ cannot do at all). Private encrypted vault vs CLZ's shared cloud. Bill Woo's Kirby covers won't fit in CLZ — they're original art, not barcoded issues.

### iCollect Comic Books
- **Pricing:** Free tier (limited) + Pro (paid). iOS-focused.
- Similar to CLZ — catalog and value tracking. No CoA. No provenance.

### ComicArtFans (CAF)
- **Pricing:** $75/year Premium
- **Strengths:** Community, social gallery, auction data, 22-year track record, 15,000+ active collectors.
- **Weaknesses:** Entirely public. No private vault. No CoA. No insurance documentation. Manual entry only.
- **Positioning:** Vaulted is not competing with CAF. It's the private documentation layer underneath CAF. (See separate competitive brief.)

### Key Collector Comics / League of Comic Geeks
- Tracking and community apps — no documentation features.

## CATEGORY 3 — TRADING CARDS / SPORTS CARDS

### CollX
- **Pricing:** Free (basic) / $10/month or $100/year Pro
- **Strengths:** 20M+ card database, visual AI scanning, portfolio value tracking, has a marketplace. Largest community among card-scanning apps. Most direct technology competitor in terms of AI photo recognition.
- **Weaknesses:** No certificate of authenticity. No private vault encryption. No multi-category support (cards only). Marketplace = privacy concern for high-value collectors. No provenance/documentation for legal purposes.
- **Vaulted's edge:** CoA generation. Multi-category (cards + comics + art + memorabilia). Private vault. Pre-grading documentation package (CGC/PSA submission prep).

### Ludex
- **Pricing:** Free (unclear premium tier)
- **Strengths:** Claims highest accuracy for parallels/variants. Good for serious Pokemon/MTG collectors.
- **Weaknesses:** Same as CollX — no CoA, no documentation, no vault.

### TCGPlayer / Dragon Shield Card Codex / Shiny
- Marketplace, casual tracking, or single-game focused. None offer CoA or provenance documentation.

### The Card Grading Services (PSA, BGS, CGC)
- These are THE authority in card authentication but they're physical grading services — weeks of turnaround time, $15–$150+ per card.
- Vaulted's angle: Pre-submission documentation. "Before you send it to PSA, vault it. Your photo record is your insurance."

## CATEGORY 4 — MEMORABILIA / SIGNED ITEMS

### No App Exists
This is the biggest gap. There is no dedicated mobile app for documenting and generating certificates for signed memorabilia.

Authentication is currently handled by physical services:
- **PSA/DNA** — industry standard for autographs. Physical submission, weeks of processing.
- **JSA (James Spence Authentication)** — major competitor to PSA, millions of verified autographs.
- **Beckett Authentication (BAS)** — strong in modern sports memorabilia.
- **CGC Signature Series** — for witnessed comic signings.

None of these are apps. None of them create a private digital vault. None let you document your own collection before professional authentication.

**Vaulted's angle here is completely uncontested:**
A collector with a Joe Montana signed jersey, a Lebron signed ball, and a Stan Lee signed comic has NO app to document all three in one place, attach photos, generate a personal certificate, and export it for insurance purposes. They're using a shoebox and prayer.

## DAY 1 MUST-HAVES

These are non-negotiable. If any of these are missing, Vaulted loses to a competitor that does have it.

### 🔴 CRITICAL — Ship these or don't launch

**1. Photo → Auto-Catalog Entry**
What it is: Take one photo, system fills in the catalog entry automatically.
Why critical: This is the core differentiator from every art app on the market. Artwork Archive requires manual entry. If Vaulted requires manual entry too, there's no reason to switch.

**2. Certificate of Authenticity — PDF Export**
What it is: A real, printable, shareable PDF certificate for any item in the vault.
Why critical: This is the #1 feature missing from every catalog and card app. It's the whole reason collectors need Vaulted over CLZ or CollX. Without it, you're just another inventory app.

**3. Barcode / QR Code Scanning**
What it is: Point camera at a barcode, item is identified and pre-filled.
Why critical: CLZ has 99% barcode accuracy for comics. Collectors expect this. If you can't do it, comics collectors won't switch from CLZ.

**4. Document / Certificate Scanning (OCR)**
What it is: Photograph an existing paper CoA, PSA cert, or label — system reads the text and imports it automatically.
Why critical: Collectors already have paper certificates. They need to digitize them. This bridges the "before Vaulted" and "after Vaulted" era of their collection.

**5. Clean Archive Photo (Background Removal)**
What it is: Photo background is removed automatically — every catalog entry looks professional.
Why critical: This is a visible, immediate differentiator. The moment someone sees a clean white-background catalog photo generated from their photo of a painting on a cluttered wall, they get it. It's wow-moment #1 in the product demo.

**6. Private Encrypted Vault**
What it is: Records are yours and not publicly visible. Nobody can see your collection without your permission.
Why critical: This is the explicit contrast to CAF and social catalog apps. "Your vault is private" is a core promise, especially for high-value collections.

**7. Free Tier (25 Items)**
What it is: Fully functional free plan with a hard limit.
Why critical: Zero-friction tryout. With no ad budget, every acquisition depends on people trying it and converting. A 14-day trial gets abandoned. A permanent free tier gets used.

**8. Multi-Category Support**
What it is: Art + Comics + Cards + Memorabilia all in one app, with category-appropriate fields.
Why critical: The cross-category collector is Vaulted's unique user. No one else serves them. Launching with only art support abandons the comics and card angle entirely.

**9. Inventory Export (Excel/CSV)**
What it is: Download your full collection as a spreadsheet.
Why critical: Insurance companies and estate planners need this. It's the #1 practical output collectors want beyond the CoA. Also removes fear of lock-in — "I can export my data anytime."
