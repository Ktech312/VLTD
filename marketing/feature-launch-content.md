When you're scrolling through 200 cards, you don't want to hunt for the grade in a text field. You want to see it.

## 9. Social Export Share

### LinkedIn Post
Your collection deserves to look as good as it is.

**Social Export Share** on VLTD generates a polished, shareable image for any item in your vault — item photo, name, value (optional), grade, and hashtags, all composed into a card that looks native on Instagram, Twitter, or TikTok.

No screenshots. No cropping. No manual captioning.

Choose your aspect ratio, pick your color scheme, toggle the value on or off, and tap Share. The image goes straight to your social app.

Your vault, finally shareable.

→ vltd.app

#collector #newpickup #hobbylife #sportscards #VLTD

---

### X/Twitter Thread
🧵 Social Export Share just shipped on VLTD.

This is the feature that turns your vault into content 👇

1/ You can now generate a polished share card for any item in your VLTD vault. Item image, name, grade, value (optional), and hashtags — composed and ready to post.

2/ Multiple aspect ratios: square for IG grid, 9:16 for Stories/TikTok, 4:5 for portrait. Your pick.

3/ Hashtags are auto-generated from your item's universe, category, title keywords, and grade. You see the full list, toggle any tag on or off, add your own.

4/ The image goes to the Web Share API — directly into your Photos app, Instagram, Twitter, or wherever you want to drop it. One tap from vault to feed.

5/ Value display is optional. You control what shows up. No surprises.

6/ Social Export Share is live on vltd.app. Open any item, tap the share icon.

#collector #hobbylife #newpickup #pokemontcg #VLTD

---

### Changelog Entry
**Social Export Share** — Canvas-based social image generator for vault items. Generates PNG exports with item image, name, grade chip, optional value display, and hashtags. Supports square (1:1), portrait (4:5), and story (9:16) aspect ratios. Auto-generates hashtags from universe, category, title keywords, and grade with a chip-picker UI for editing. Exports via Web Share API (mobile) or direct download (desktop).
