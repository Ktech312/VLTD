## PLATFORM GUIDE

**Instagram** — Visual, collector community, short punchy hooks, personal/emotional angle.
**LinkedIn** — Founder voice, market insight, building-in-public updates, thoughtful longer posts.

Both platforms should feel like they come from the same person who genuinely loves this stuff — not a marketing department.

## CONTENT RULES — STAY IN BOUNDS

Always:
- Warm, curious, collector-first voice
- Specific over generic (say "Base Set Charizard" not "popular cards")
- Community feels welcoming, not exclusive
- Share real features when relevant — don't oversell what isn't live

Never:
- "Prove" anything / "documentation" as a hook
- Make collectors feel bad about how they currently track things
- Force terminology on people ("vault" is fine; don't over-brand every sentence)
- Recycle captions or hooks across posts
