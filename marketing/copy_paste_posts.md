### LINKEDIN

> What does VLTD actually do? Here's the honest version.
>
> You add items to your vault — manually or via scan. The app organizes them by universe: sports cards, TCG, comics, vinyl, watches, games, art, memorabilia.
>
> From your vault, you can:
> — Build curated exhibitions, like a personal museum. Set them public, invite-only, or keep them private.
> — Track collection goals — set completions, milestones, items you're working toward.
> — See portfolio analytics — your collection's value over time, broken down by universe.
> — Follow your activity feed — value changes, sales history, exhibition comments.
> — Discover other collectors — browse public vaults by universe, find people who love what you love.
> — Keep a wishlist — the pieces you're watching and chasing.
> — Import from Excel — your existing list doesn't disappear when you join.
>
> Private vault by default. You control what's visible and to whom.
>
> This is what we're building. No funding. No team. Just the belief that collectors deserve a real home base.
