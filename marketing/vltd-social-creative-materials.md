## Brand Voice

Confident, collector-native, premium, practical. Avoid hype language. VLTD should sound like it understands both the emotional side of collecting and the asset-management side.

## Format Checklist

Vertical video:

- 1080x1920
- Keep text away from top and bottom UI zones
- Use 3 to 5 words per overlay
- Open with a physical collectible, not a generic app screen

Feed carousel:

- 1080x1350 or 1080x1080
- One idea per slide
- High contrast text
- End with one CTA

Static image:

- Make one object the hero
- Keep the app visible when possible
- Use gold light and charcoal backgrounds
- Keep copy short enough to read at phone size
