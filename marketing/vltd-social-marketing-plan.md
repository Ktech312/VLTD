# VLTD Social Media Marketing Plan

Visual anchor: `public/brand/vltd-collector-vault-hero-app-screen-bright.png`

## Positioning

VLTD is the premium digital home for serious collectors. The brand should feel like a private vault, a collector's desk, and a modern portfolio tool in one frame.

Core line:

> Your collection deserves a real home.

Supporting angles:

- Stop treating your collection like a spreadsheet.
- Turn every collectible into a documented asset.
- Build a vault for cards, comics, music, art, memorabilia, and the pieces only you understand.
- Scan, organize, value, present, and share your collection from one place.

## Audience

Primary:

- Collectors with mixed collections: cards, comics, figures, vinyl, instruments, posters, memorabilia.
- Collectors who care about value, insurance, organization, and presentation.
- Sellers and hobbyists who need cleaner records than camera roll folders.

Secondary:

- Family collectors managing inherited collections.
- High-value hobbyists who want a portfolio-style view.
- Content creators who showcase collectibles.

## Campaign Theme

Name: `The Collection Deserves Better`

Creative world:

- Matte black collector desk
- Warm gold rim light
- Premium acrylic slabs, framed pieces, instruments, vinyl, figures
- Tablet or laptop showing the VLTD vault
- Short, confident text overlays
- No loud e-commerce look, no clutter, no cheap stock-photo energy

## 30-Day Channel Plan

Cadence:

- Instagram: 4 posts per week, 3 Reels, 1 carousel
- TikTok: 3 short videos per week
- YouTube Shorts: 2 short videos per week
- X: 4 short posts per week
- Facebook Groups/Page: 2 collector-focused posts per week
- LinkedIn: 1 founder/product post per week for the asset-management angle

Week 1: Problem

- Theme: collections scattered across photos, spreadsheets, notes, and memory.
- CTA: Join the vault / start your collection profile.
- Best formats: Reels, TikTok, static quote cards.

Week 2: Product

- Theme: scan, organize, value, and present.
- CTA: Build your first vault.
- Best formats: screen walkthroughs, carousel explainers.

Week 3: Collection Types

- Theme: cards, comics, figures, vinyl, instruments, memorabilia.
- CTA: Show us what is in your vault.
- Best formats: category-specific posts and user prompts.

Week 4: Trust and Status

- Theme: insurance readiness, collection value, public galleries, serious collector identity.
- CTA: Upgrade your collection from hobby pile to managed portfolio.
- Best formats: proof posts, founder POV, before/after.

## Content Pillars

1. Vault Identity

   Make VLTD feel aspirational: a private digital vault for collectors.

2. Utility

   Show practical workflows: scan, catalog, value, export, insure, share.

3. Collection Culture

   Speak to collector emotion: the hunt, the grails, the stories, the weird categories.

4. Trust

   Position collection records as useful for resale, insurance, family planning, and organization.

5. Showcase

   Use beautiful collection scenes and app screenshots to make the product tangible.

## Core Formats

Vertical short video:

- 9:16, 1080x1920
- 8 to 20 seconds
- Large center-safe text
- First frame must show either a premium collection scene or a messy-before hook

Carousel:

- 4:5 feed-friendly slides
- 5 to 7 cards
- One idea per card
- End with a clear CTA

Static post:

- Hero image crop with short headline
- Minimal text
- Strong object detail

## Paid Test Plan

Initial test budget: small creative validation before scaling.

Campaign groups:

- Collector pain: scattered collection, no records, hard to track value.
- Premium vault: aspirational lifestyle and collection presentation.
- Insurance/value: documentation and portfolio angle.
- Category-specific: sports cards, comics, vinyl/music, figures.

Creative tests:

- A: "Your collection deserves a real home."
- B: "Stop losing value in camera roll chaos."
- C: "The vault for everything you collect."
- D: "From hobby pile to collection portfolio."

Landing destination:

- Send cold traffic to the strongest product page or signup page.
- Send retargeting traffic to a more direct "Start your vault" CTA.

## Measurement

Track weekly:

- Hook rate: 3-second video views / impressions
- Hold rate: average watch time
- Save/share rate on educational posts
- Profile clicks
- Signup conversion
- Cost per signup for paid tests

Creative decisions:

- Keep concepts with high saves, comments, or signups.
- Recut videos with good hooks but weak completion.
- Retire posts that get impressions but no profile clicks.

## Production Notes

Use the bright hero style as the master art direction:

- Keep warm gold light on the right or behind objects.
- Keep the product interface visible but not tiny.
- Use real collectible categories in the frame.
- Keep overlays short and high contrast.
- Avoid tiny UI copy in social crops.
