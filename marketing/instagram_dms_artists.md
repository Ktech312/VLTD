# Vaulted — Instagram DM Scripts for Artists

---

## KEY RULES FOR ARTIST DMs

1. **No AI language.** Not once. Not even hinting at it.
2. **Lead with their work, not yours.** Comment on something they made before you DM.
3. **Keep it short.** Artists get DM spam constantly. 3–4 sentences max for the first message.
4. **Be honest that you built it.** "I made this" lands better than "I found this tool."
5. **Don't ask them to sign up in the first message.** Ask if they're interested first.
6. **Always engage with their content first** — like recent posts, leave a real comment — before DMing. Wait at least a day.

---

## STEP 1 — The Comment (Before You DM)

Leave a genuine comment on one of their recent pieces. Be specific — reference the actual work.

Examples:
> "The texture on this one is incredible — is that palette knife work?"

> "I love how you handled the light in this. The shadow detail is doing a lot."

> "This one stopped me mid-scroll. Really striking composition."

Don't say "nice work" or "amazing." Be specific. Artists notice when someone actually looked.

## HOW TO HANDLE REPLIES

**"What is it exactly?"**
> It's a documentation tool for your artwork. You photograph a piece, it automatically builds a catalog record — pulls in subject matter, cleans the background, reads any certificates or labels you include — and generates a real certificate of authenticity you can export as a PDF. Everything's stored securely in your own private vault.

**"Is this AI?" or "Does this use AI art?"**
> No — it doesn't generate any art at all. It documents yours. Think of it like a smart scanner — it reads your photo and builds a record of what's in it. Your work stays completely yours. The tool just creates the paper trail that proves it.

*(Note: This is the honest answer. Vaulted doesn't generate art, it documents it. You're not misleading anyone — you're just leading with what matters to them.)*

**"How much does it cost?"**
> Free to start. Pro is $9/month when billing turns on. But founding artists — the first 50 people — get Pro free while they're actively giving feedback.

**"I'm interested"**
> Amazing — I'll send you the link. You're [#X] of 50. Welcome aboard.
> Would love to hear what you think once you've tried it. Honest feedback only, good or bad.

**"I don't have time right now"**
> Totally get it. I'll drop the link here in case you want to check it out later. No pressure at all.
> [link]

**No reply after 3–5 days**
> Don't follow up. Move on. If they come back to it later, great.

The poll drives engagement and tells you exactly what you're working with as an audience.
