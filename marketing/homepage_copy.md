## HOW IT WORKS SECTION

**Section headline:**
> Three steps. 90 seconds. A record that lasts forever.

**Step 1**
🎨 **Shoot**
Photograph your piece with any camera. Scan barcodes or QR codes on existing labels. Snap your certificate of authenticity if you have one.

**Step 2**
🤖 **AI Catalogs**
Our AI identifies subject matter, reads text from any documents you attach, removes the background for a clean archive photo, and creates a unique digital fingerprint for the piece.

**Step 3**
🔒 **Vault It**
Your record is encrypted and stored in your personal vault — accessible anytime, exportable as a PDF certificate of authenticity or an Excel inventory report.
