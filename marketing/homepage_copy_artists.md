## HOW IT WORKS SECTION

**Headline:**
> Three steps. 90 seconds. A record that lasts forever.

**Step 1 — Shoot**
Photograph your piece with any camera. Scan barcodes or QR codes if your piece already has labels. Snap your certificate of authenticity if you have one.

**Step 2 — Document**
Vaulted automatically builds your catalog entry — subject matter, medium, and a clean archive photo with the background removed. Any certificates or notes you include get pulled in automatically too.

**Step 3 — Vault It**
Your record is encrypted and stored in your personal vault — yours to access anytime. Export a PDF certificate of authenticity or a full inventory spreadsheet whenever you need it.
