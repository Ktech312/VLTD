Wishlist, Pull List and Following

Proposed layout for the Watchlist page (the address and the menu links stay the same). Everything below is sample data made up for the layout; the series, products and prices are not real.

WishlistWhat do I want? Anything, in any category, with or without a date or price.
Pull ListWhat am I expecting, and when? Wants that have a release date or window, grouped by when.
FollowingWhat future releases should VLTD collect for me? Rules for a series, a card product or a toy line.
