# Narration script: Add your first item

Landscape tutorial · 1:37 · caption-led (no voice-over recorded). Read at an unhurried pace.

**What you need:** A photo of the item on your device; About 2 minutes.

**[0:00] Intro** — Add your first item. Upload a photo, name it, choose a category, add optional details, save and reopen it.

**[0:06] 1. Open Add Item** — Start in your Vault. Choose Add Item, at the top right.

**[0:17] 2. Choose Manual entry** — From the menu, pick Manual entry so you can type the details yourself.

**[0:27] 3. Add a photo** — Upload a photo from your device. It appears in the photo strip.

**[0:38] 4. Name your item** — Type a title. When the form says Ready to save, you are good to go.

**[0:48] 5. Choose a category and add details** — Choose a universe and a category. Series, purchase price and current value are optional, so add what you know.

**[0:59] 6. Save** — Choose Save. A green Saved banner confirms it.

**[1:09] 7. Find it in your Vault** — Back in your Vault, the item is there with its photo, category and value.

**[1:20] 8. Open it any time** — Open the item any time to see its photo, details, value and gain.

**[1:30] Wrap-up** — Your first item is saved to your Vault. Next: build an exhibition from your items.
