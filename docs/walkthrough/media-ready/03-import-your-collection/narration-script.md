# Narration script: Import your collection

Landscape tutorial · 1:17 · caption-led (no voice-over recorded). Read at an unhurried pace.

**What you need:** A CSV or Excel file with a header row; One item per row; About 3 minutes.

**[0:00] Intro** — Import your collection. Choose a spreadsheet, check the mapping and warnings, review duplicates, confirm and see your items.

**[0:06] 1. Open Import** — In your Vault, open Tools and choose Import.

**[0:13] 2. Choose your spreadsheet** — Choose Upload Spreadsheet and pick your CSV or Excel file.

**[0:21] 3. Check the summary** — Check the summary: items ready, total cost, total value and anything that needs a look.

**[0:30] 4. Review duplicates and warnings** — Exact duplicates are listed here, and anything unclear is flagged rather than guessed.

**[0:39] 5. Check the column mapping** — Confirm each of your columns is mapped to the right field.

**[0:46] 6. Handle duplicates and confirm** — Choose how to handle duplicates, then choose Review and Confirm Import.

**[0:54] 7. See what was imported** — VLTD tells you what was imported and what was skipped.

**[1:03] 8. See your items** — Your imported items are now in your Vault.

**[1:10] Wrap-up** — Your spreadsheet items are in your Vault. Next: add photos to your imported items.
