# Narration script: Build and share an exhibition

Landscape tutorial · 1:44 · caption-led (no voice-over recorded). Read at an unhurried pace.

**What you need:** At least three items in your Vault; About 3 minutes.

**[0:00] Intro** — Build and share an exhibition. Create an exhibition, choose items, save, preview it, pick who can see it and share it.

**[0:06] 1. Create First Exhibition** — Open Exhibitions and choose Create First Exhibition.

**[0:14] 2. Title and description** — Give your exhibition a title and a short description.

**[0:21] 3. Choose who can see it** — Start with Private while you build. You can open it up later.

**[0:28] 4. Create Exhibition** — Then choose Create Exhibition.

**[0:35] 5. Your exhibition page** — Your exhibition is created and private, ready to fill.

**[0:42] 6. Edit/Add** — Choose Edit and Add to pick items from your Vault.

**[0:50] 7. Choose items** — Tick the items you want. A tick shows each item that is selected.

**[0:58] 8. Add to Exhibit** — Choose Add to Exhibit. The number shows how many items you picked.

**[1:05] 9. Review and save** — Your items sit on the shelf. Choose Save.

**[1:13] 10. Preview as a visitor** — Open the guest preview to see exactly what visitors will see.

**[1:21] 11. Choose visibility** — When you are ready, choose Public Exhibit and confirm Make public.

**[1:29] 12. Share it** — The exhibition is now public. Choose Copy Link to copy the visitor link, or use a share button.

**[1:37] Wrap-up** — Your exhibition is public and ready to share. Next: import more of your collection with a spreadsheet.
