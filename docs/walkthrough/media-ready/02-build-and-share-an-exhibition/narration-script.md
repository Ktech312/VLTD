# Narration script: Build and share an exhibition

Landscape tutorial · 1:58 · caption-led (no voice-over recorded). Read at an unhurried pace.

**What you need:** At least three items in your Vault; About 3 minutes.

**[0:00] Intro** — Build and share an exhibition. Create an exhibition, choose items, save, preview it, pick who can see it and share it.

**[0:06] 1. Create First Exhibition** — Open Exhibitions and choose Create First Exhibition.

**[0:17] 2. Title and description** — Give your exhibition a title and a short description.

**[0:27] 3. Choose who can see it** — Start with Private while you build. Then choose Create Exhibition.

**[0:38] 4. Your exhibition page** — Your exhibition is created and private, ready to fill.

**[0:48] 5. Choose items** — Choose Edit and Add. Your Vault items appear so you can pick from them.

**[0:59] 6. Add them** — Tick the items you want, then choose Add to Exhibit.

**[1:09] 7. Review and save** — Your items sit on the shelf with their total value. Choose Save.

**[1:20] 8. Preview as a visitor** — Open the guest preview to see exactly what visitors will see.

**[1:30] 9. Choose visibility** — When you are ready, choose Public Exhibit and confirm Make public.

**[1:41] 10. Share it** — The exhibition is now public. Use Copy Link or the share buttons to send it.

**[1:51] Wrap-up** — Your exhibition is public and ready to share. Next: import more of your collection with a spreadsheet.
