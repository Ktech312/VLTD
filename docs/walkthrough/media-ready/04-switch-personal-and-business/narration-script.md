# Narration script: Switch between Personal and Business

Landscape tutorial · 1:08 · caption-led (no voice-over recorded). Read at an unhurried pace.

**What you need:** A VLTD account; A business name and handle; About 2 minutes.

**[0:00] Intro** — Switch between Personal and Business. Create a Business profile, switch between profiles and see that each keeps its own collection.

**[0:06] 1. Add a business profile** — Open Settings and choose Add business profile.

**[0:14] 2. Enter the details** — Enter a business name and handle, choose a business type, then create the profile.

**[0:23] 3. You are in the Business profile** — Your Business profile is created, and Settings shows it as active.

**[0:30] 4. A separate, empty Vault** — The Business profile starts with its own empty Vault.

**[0:37] 5. Add items to this profile** — Anything you add or import here stays in the Business profile.

**[0:44] 6. Switch profile** — To switch, open the account menu and choose a profile under Switch profile.

**[0:53] 7. Your Personal Vault is unchanged** — Your Personal Vault shows only your personal items. The two collections stay separate.

**[1:01] Wrap-up** — Personal and Business collections stay separate. Next: add an item to either profile.
