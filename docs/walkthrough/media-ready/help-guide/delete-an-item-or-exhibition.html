Delete an item or an exhibition

Deleting is permanent. VLTD always asks you to confirm first.

Delete an item

  1. Open the item from your Vault.
  2. Choose the trash icon (Delete item).
  3. Read the warning, then choose Delete Forever, or Cancel to keep it.

Delete an exhibition

  1. Open Exhibitions.
  2. On the exhibition's card, choose the trash icon (Delete exhibition).
  3. Choose Yes Delete FOREVER, or Cancel, keep My Exhibition.
If your account cannot be reached VLTD says so and keeps the item. Nothing is shown as deleted unless it was.
