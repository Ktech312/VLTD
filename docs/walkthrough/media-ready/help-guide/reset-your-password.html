Reset your password

You can set a new password from the email we send you.

  1. On the log-in page choose Forgot password?.
  2. Enter your email and choose Send reset link. The message is the same whether or not an account exists.
  3. Open the email "Reset your VLTD password" and choose Reset my password.
  4. Type your new password twice and choose Update password.
  5. Log in with the new password. Your old password stops working, and other devices are signed out.
"This link has expired" Reset links work once and only for a short time. Choose Send me a new link to get another.
