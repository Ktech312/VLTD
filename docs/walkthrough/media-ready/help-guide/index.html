Add your first item

Upload a photo, name it, choose a category, add optional details, save and reopen it.

Video, 1:37, captions on screen. Caption file

Before you start

  • A photo of the item on your device
  • About 2 minutes
1

Open Add Item

In your Vault, choose ADD ITEM.

2

Choose Manual entry

Pick Manual entry to type the details yourself.

3

Add a photo

Upload a photo from your device. It appears in the photo strip.

4

Name your item

Type a title. The form shows Ready to save.

5

Choose a category and add details

Pick a Universe and Category. Series, purchase price and current value are optional.

6

Save

Choose Save. You will see a green Saved! banner.

7

Find it in your Vault

Your item is in the Vault with its photo, category and value.

8

Open it any time

Open the item to see its photo, details, value and gain.

Done

Your first item is saved to your Vault.

Next: build an exhibition from your items.

Build and share an exhibition

Create an exhibition, choose items, save, preview it, pick who can see it and share it.

Video, 1:58, captions on screen. Caption file

Before you start

  • At least three items in your Vault
  • About 3 minutes
1

Create First Exhibition

Open Exhibitions and choose Create First Exhibition.

2

Title and description

Give it a title and a short description.

3

Choose who can see it

Start with Private while you build. Then choose Create Exhibition.

4

Your exhibition page

Your exhibition is created, private and ready to fill.

5

Choose items

Choose Edit/Add. Your Vault items appear to pick from.

6

Add them

Tick the items you want, then choose Add to Exhibit.

7

Review and save

Your items sit on the shelf with their total value. Choose Save.

8

Preview as a visitor

Open the guest preview to see what visitors will see.

9

Choose visibility

Choose Public Exhibit, then confirm Make public.

10

Share it

The exhibition is Public. Use Copy Link or the share buttons.

Done

Your exhibition is public and ready to share.

Next: import more of your collection with a spreadsheet.

Import your collection

Choose a spreadsheet, check the mapping and warnings, review duplicates, confirm and see your items.

Video, 1:37, captions on screen. Caption file

Before you start

  • A CSV or Excel file with a header row
  • One item per row
  • About 3 minutes
1

Open Import

In your Vault, open Tools and choose Import.

2

Choose your spreadsheet

Choose Upload Spreadsheet and pick your CSV or Excel file.

3

Check the summary

See items ready, total cost, total value and anything that needs a look.

4

Review duplicates and warnings

Exact duplicates are listed. Nothing unclear is guessed.

5

Check the column mapping

Confirm each column is mapped to the right field.

6

Choose how to handle duplicates

For example, skip exact duplicate titles within this batch.

7

Confirm

Choose Review and Confirm Import, then confirm. You will see what was imported and skipped.

8

See your items

Your imported items are now in your Vault.

Done

Your spreadsheet items are in your Vault.

Next: add photos to your imported items.

Switch between Personal and Business

Create a Business profile, switch between profiles and see that each keeps its own collection.

Video, 1:26, captions on screen. Caption file

Before you start

  • A VLTD account
  • A business name and handle
  • About 2 minutes
1

Add a business profile

In Settings, choose + Add business profile.

2

Enter the details

Enter a business name and handle, then choose a business type.

3

You are in the Business profile

Settings now shows your Business profile as active.

4

A separate, empty Vault

The Business profile starts with its own empty Vault.

5

Add items to this profile

Items you add or import here stay in the Business profile.

6

Switch profile

Open the account menu and choose a profile under Switch profile.

7

Your Personal Vault is unchanged

Your Personal Vault shows only your personal items.

Done

Personal and Business collections stay separate.
