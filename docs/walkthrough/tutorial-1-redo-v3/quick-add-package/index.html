What the promo uses

  • Phone captures (Home, camera, review, Verify sheet) with the same highlights as the how-to.
  • One landscape crop of the verified desktop Vault screen (the three new items under their filter chips) as the "organized result". The phone Vault capture does not show the item cards.
  • The platinum VLTD logo from the site's brand folder on the end card, and a plain call to action. No new footage was captured.

What is new, and what is reused

  • Reused unchanged: every screenshot, the caption wording, the highlight style (one highlight at a time, sliding between controls) and the dark caption styling. The desktop how-to is the approved video with the phone section cut out.
  • Phone how-to, new layout only: portrait frame, the phone screen at full height, large caption text underneath. Five extra highlight positions (Universe and Category buttons, the Items count, and fields on the review and Verify sheets) were read from the same phone screenshots; the ring was checked against the picture in every case. No screen was recaptured for this package.
  • Phone review step: the phone review screenshot lists items 1 to 3; the accidental fourth photo is scrolled out of view, so the caption says "scroll to find a bad shot". The review shows item 3 (the toy) as TCG, and the next step fixes it, matching the Verify sheet where it reads Pop Culture / Toys and Figures.
  • Phone ending: the phone Vault screenshot shows Items 3 at the top, not the item cards, so the video ends there and says to scroll down.
  • Length: the phone how-to is 1:26, shorter than the desktop how-to (1:58) because the phone capture set has fewer screens (no Universe or Category menus, no saved message, no opened item). I did not recapture to pad it.

Honesty and checks

  • Made from screenshots of the live app with sample items and a test camera that shows fictional cards. They are not recordings of a person using the app. The browser's camera-permission prompt cannot be captured, so it is a card. The phone screens come from a phone-sized test browser.
  • No voice and no audio track in any file.
  • No AI is shown or implied anywhere in the promo or the how-to cuts. The words "scan", "identify" and "instantly" do not appear.
  • Phone readability: the portrait videos are 1080 px wide. Shown full width on a 390-point phone, captions are about 19 pt (how-to) and 33 pt (promo headlines), titles about 25 pt, and the small section label about 12 pt. The landscape videos are meant for a computer screen.
  • Nothing was published. No app change, no paid service, no live Help change.
