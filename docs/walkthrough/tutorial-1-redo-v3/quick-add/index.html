Exactly what changed from v2

  1. Starts on Home. The video opens on Home with the Quick Add button highlighted (nav tabs in view so it reads as Home), with the tutorial title shown briefly over it. The button is then pressed with a ripple, and the camera follows. v2 showed the same Home screenshot but with both buttons ringed at once, and it came after a long intro.
  2. One highlight at a time. v2 drew every ring on a screen together with numbers. Now only the control being taught is lit, the rest of the screen is dimmed, and the ring slides to the next control on the same screen. The numbers 1 to 4 are used only for the camera set-up order (Universe 1, Category 2, shutter 3, Finished 4). Each is followed by the screen it produces. This is the camera sample's highlight style.
  3. Holds shortened. v2 held each screen 8 to 26 seconds. Now a screen is held for the time to read its caption plus about 2 seconds: 3.2 to 6.7 seconds per screen (the shortest is the button press), and 4 to 8 seconds for the three cards. The video went from 8:40 to 2:25.
  4. Captions simplified. One short sentence per screen, 15 words or fewer (26 of 27 are 14 or fewer); the step counter and the long second line are gone.
  5. Save is now highlighted. Same Verify screenshot as v2 (the button is visible there); the ring position was measured from the live app's page layout.
  6. Screens left out of this video (still in v2): Vault entry points, the four empty-Vault starting points, Upload from device, Add manually, and the phone review screen. They are not part of the Home to saved-item path.
  7. Kept from v2 and shortened: camera permission (one card), the blocked-camera message, review and remove, category fix, names, cost, Vault search, open item, phone differences (5 screens), recap.
  8. Framing: camera and dialog screens now show the whole camera column or the whole dialog, so nothing is cut off. Home is cropped to the nav tabs plus the top card.
  9. No audio. This file has no audio track. The yellow production notes (the "Simulated" lines and the "Explanation card" label) are removed from the picture; the disclosure is on this page and in the verification record, not in the frames.
  10. Smoother ring movement: 12 frames per second instead of 8.
  11. One screen recaptured. The phone "Name, then save" picture is a new capture (the toy now reads Pop Culture / Toys & Figures). Every other picture is one of v2's verified screenshots.

Voice

There is no voice in this version. I cannot listen to audio, so I cannot check a computer voice for clicks or popping myself, and I will not ship one I could not check. Captions carry the explanation. If you want a voice later, it would need a way to verify it that does not rely on my ears (for example a measured check for sudden jumps in the waveform), and your approval first.
