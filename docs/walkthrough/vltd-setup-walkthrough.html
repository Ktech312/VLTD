VLTD setup walkthrough

Picking an avatar, creating your personal profile, adding a business profile, and switching between them. Screenshots were taken on the live site with a throwaway test account (deleted afterwards).

Part 1 - Set up your personal account

1

Start on the setup page

After you sign in for the first time, VLTD opens "Your personal account." Four bars at the top show how far along you are.

2

Pick an avatar and type your name

Tap any of the round pictures. The one you picked gets a white ring (here, the second row, first picture). Then type your Display name and a Username. The Username becomes your public link, vltd.app/@username. The box stopped taking letters at 24 characters. Tap "Save and continue."

3

Your profile is saved right away

The green box confirms your personal profile is saved to your account, so you can carry on from another device. Now choose: "Personal only" (the default) or "Add a business profile" (a separate profile for a shop, dealer, brand or team). Then tap Continue.

4

Pick your look

Choose Classic or Bright, and Dark or Light. You can change this later in the account menu. Tap Continue.

5

Choose what you collect, then finish

Tap the universes you collect in (or "Skip - show everything"). The setup summary at the bottom lists exactly which profiles will be created. Tap "Finish setup."

6

You land on Home

Your vault is ready and shows your name. If you refresh the page at any point during setup, you pick up where you left off.

Part 2 - Add a business profile later

7

Open Account, then Profile

From the account menu choose Settings. In the Profile section, tap "+ Add business profile."

8

The business form

Your personal profile stays separate. This form creates another profile for a shop, dealer, brand, estate or team.

9

Fill it in and create it

Business name and Business handle are required. Business type, Website and EIN / Tax ID are optional, and the tax ID is private. Tap "Create business profile." (The tax ID shown here is a made-up test number.)

10

You are now working as the business

VLTD switches you to the new business profile straight away. The header reads "Walkthrough Shop - Business" and a new "Workspace and team" section appears.

Part 3 - Switch between personal and business

11

Open the profile list

Tap the profile name at the top right of Settings. Both profiles are listed, with a check mark on the active one. (The account menu in the top bar also has a "Switch profile" list.)

12

Pick the other profile

Choose your personal profile and the page changes to it: name, handle and the "Personal profile" box.

13

Home follows the active profile

Switch back to the business and Home greets the business. The choice is remembered when you reload the page.

Part 4 - What other people see

14

The business's public page

This is what a visitor who is not signed in sees at vltd.app/@business_handle: the business name and picture, and Follow and Message buttons. It does not show the tax ID, your personal name, or your private items. Items only appear here once you mark them public.

Good to know: the tax ID is stored privately and is not shown anywhere in the app, including to you. If you refresh the page during setup, you keep your place.
