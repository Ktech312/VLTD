What kind of events these are

  • Card shows

    A hall of independent dealers renting tables for a day or a weekend. Best place to compare prices on the same card in one afternoon.

  • Tournaments

    Organised competitive play with entry fees and prizes. Expect a posted format, a start time you need to arrive before, and a deck or roster requirement.
